Introduction

What Clubhouse is, and what this blog is going to cover.

Clubhouse is a campus app. Students find events, clubs and opportunities around them, and the clubs on the other side of that run their listings, their members and their applications from the same place. This blog is the engineering account of building it: how the data is modelled, how the client is put together, how the design system stays coherent, and how recommendations decide what a given student sees first.
